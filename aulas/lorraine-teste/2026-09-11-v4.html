📌 Recapitulação: Respostas Curtas (Did, Will, Would vs Don't/Didn't)
A Regra do Espelhamento: O mesmo verbo auxiliar usado na pergunta deve ser usado na resposta curta.
• Passado (Did): Did you forget? ➔ No, I didn't. (NUNCA use "don't" no passado!)
• Presente (Do): Do you need to pay? ➔ No, I don't.
• Futuro (Will): Will you rain? ➔ No, it won't.
• Condicional (Would): Would you mind? ➔ No, I wouldn't.
